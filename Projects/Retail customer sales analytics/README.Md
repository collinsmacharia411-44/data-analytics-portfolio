# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket sales data to identify sales patterns, evaluate product performance, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and generate actionable business insights.

The objective is to demonstrate practical data analytics skills through data preparation, SQL analysis, data visualization, and business interpretation.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall supermarket sales performance.
- Identify the highest and lowest performing product lines.
- Compare sales performance across cities.
- Analyze member versus normal customer purchasing behaviour.
- Examine customer payment preferences.
- Identify trends and opportunities for business improvement.

## 3. Dataset

The project uses the Supermarket Sales dataset, containing 1,000 transactions and 17 columns.

Key fields include:
- Invoice ID
- Branch and City
- Customer Type and Gender
- Product Line
- Unit Price and Quantity
- Sales and Gross Income
- Date and Time
- Payment Method
- Customer Rating

## 6. Key Business Insights

### Product Performance
- Food and beverages recorded the highest sales at 56,144.84.
- Health and beauty recorded the lowest sales at 49,193.74.
- Product performance was relatively balanced across the six product lines.

### City Performance
- Naypyitaw generated the highest sales at 110,568.71.
- Yangon generated 106,200.37.
- Mandalay generated 106,197.67.

### Customer Analysis
- Members generated 189,694.76 in sales.
- Normal customers generated 133,271.98.
- Members contributed approximately 58.7% of total sales.

### Payment Analysis
- Cash generated 112,206.57 in sales.
- Ewallet generated 109,993.11.
- Credit cards generated 100,767.07.

### Day-of-Week Performance
- Saturday recorded the highest sales at 56,120.81.
- Monday recorded the lowest sales at 37,899.08.

## 7. Data Quality

The dataset was checked for:
- Duplicate invoice IDs.
- Missing values in key fields.
- Invalid or non-positive sales and quantity values.
- Negative gross income values.

The checks identified no duplicate invoice IDs or missing values in the key fields examined.
